How it works

Drag any of the four handles to change the diameter. The shape stays circular, and the diameter controls update as you drag.

Starting at the top, each step compares a block, 1x1, 1x2, and 1x4 wedge by their mean squared error against the circle. The chosen section is mirrored to form the full shape.
